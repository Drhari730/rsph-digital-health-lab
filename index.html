What the lab works on

Four areas that match the School's teaching and research programmes.

Apps for TB, diabetes and learning, designed for Indian patients and students, and evaluated with proper study designs.

AI & healthcare analytics

Guideline-grounded chatbots, evidence synthesis, health economics and explainable models for public health decisions.

Technology in public health

Private AI for the School

Our own AI assistant, running on our own machine

Student records, exam data, interview transcripts and internal documents should not be uploaded to public AI services. We are setting up open-source language models on a campus workstation, connected to the School's own documents and fine-tuned on our teaching material. Data stays on the campus network.

  • Teaching: course tutor for MPH/MHA students, MCQ drafting for Pariksha
  • Research: assisted qualitative coding, literature screening, analysis on data that cannot leave campus
  • Administration: minutes, circulars, accreditation drafts, policy search
  • Governance: university-owned hardware, IT-controlled access, a human approves every output
